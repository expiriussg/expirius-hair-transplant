Before you decide

Clarity is part of good care.

Hair transplantation is a medical procedure with potential risks and downtime. Suitability, technique, graft planning and expected outcomes should be discussed with a qualified doctor.

Ask who will perform each stage, what credentials they hold, what aftercare is provided, and what happens if you need medical attention after returning home.

Do not make travel arrangements until you receive and accept a written treatment plan and package quotation.

04 / Common questions

Before your consultation

The practical details.

Are return flights really included?+

Both packages are intended to include return economy flights from Singapore to Istanbul, subject to the fare allowance and travel terms stated in your final written quotation. Malaysian departure arrangements may differ and will be quoted individually.

What does “nett” mean?+

It means the stated package price is intended as the total for the confirmed inclusions, without an additional service fee. Any exclusions or variable travel costs must be identified in your written quotation.

Is DHI suitable for everyone?+

No. Suitability depends on factors such as medical history, hair-loss pattern and donor area. A clinician must assess you before confirming treatment.

Are results guaranteed?+

No medical outcome can be guaranteed. Results and timelines vary by individual, and the treating doctor should explain realistic expectations and risks during consultation.
